    Charter and Meeting Plan Summary¶

    Stake Education and Enrichment Committee — Charter and Meeting Plan — One Page Summary

    Purpose¶

    This charter establishes the Education & Enrichment Committee, which delivers Part 4 of the Gathering Plan and staffs the two Co-Chair seats named in the YSA Council Charter. It defines the Committee's structure, roles, meeting rhythm, and reporting line so the Stake YSA Council can steer the vision while the Committee owns day-to-day execution.

    Committee Structure¶

    A fixed, ten-member Committee Core (all Young Single Adults), supported by advisor couples and a seasonal Facilitator & Mentor Network:

    Layer Composition Size
    Committee (Tier 1) Co-Chairs + 8 coordinators, all YSAs 10 members
    Advisor Couples Married couples per the Guide for Married Couples; non-voting 2–3 couples
    Facilitator & Mentor Network (Tier 2) Facilitators, presenters, hosts, mentors recruited each curriculum cycle 15–25, seasonal

    Ten seats: Education & Enrichment Co-Chairs (Sister & Priesthood-YSA High Councilor), Monthly Enrichment Night Producer, Small-Group Facilitator Coordinator, Workshop & Seminar Series Coordinator, Discussion Group Coordinator, Mentorship Pairing Coordinator, Institute & Covenant Progression Liaison, Learning & Enrichment Coordinator, and Committee Secretary/Metrics Recorder. Young single adults plan, run, and teach every session; advisor couples support and mentor without leading.

    New — Learning & Enrichment Coordinator: the Committee's needs-and-resources hub. Gathers members' interests, talents, and needs; keeps a stake-wide resource list of teachers, presenters, and experts; recommends 3–5 Christ-centered topics each cycle; and plans firesides and pilot workshops. Supplies topics, speakers, facilitators, mentors, and needs data to the other Coordinators and the Secretary, and hands proven pilots to the Workshop & Seminar Coordinator so the two seats never compete.

    Meeting & Reporting¶

    Meeting Frequency Purpose
    Committee Core Meeting Biweekly (first 90 days), then monthly Review curriculum calendar and needs summary; coordinate facilitators, workshops, mentors
    Needs & Resources Huddle Monthly, before Committee meeting Learning & Enrichment, Workshop, and Producer match needs to topics and presenters
    Enrichment Night Debrief Monthly Assess belonging, attendance, and the commitment invitation
    YSA Council Report Monthly Co-Chairs deliver the stewardship report, incl. member needs, to the Executive Co-Chairs
    Quarterly Curriculum Review Quarterly Progress check on the four curriculum threads and the quarter's needs patterns

    Decision Rights¶

    • Committee decides on its own: enrichment-night/workshop content and calendar; facilitator, presenter, and mentor recruitment and assignment; needs-driven workshops and firesides within the approved budget and calendar.
    • Committee recommends, Executive Co-Chairs approve: coordinator or advisor-couple changes; any paid presenter, honorarium, or spending beyond the enrichment budget; content and distribution of a stake-wide member interest survey.
    • Escalates immediately: building-use conflicts (Executive Co-Chairs resolve) and sensitive individual situations (go straight to bishops or stake presidency). Survey responses are reported as patterns, never names.

    First 90 Days¶

    Weeks 1–2: confirm charter and Advisor assignments. Weeks 2–4: call Coordinators/Secretary; recruit first facilitators; Learning & Enrichment Coordinator sets scope with the Priesthood Co-Chair. Weeks 4–6: first Committee Core meeting and Enrichment Night scheduled; leader needs gathering begins and survey submitted for approval. Weeks 6–8: first Enrichment Night runs; workshop calendar published; first resource list shared. Weeks 8–12: first stewardship report delivered; discussion groups launch; first needs summary reported and pilot workshop or fireside planned.

    Summary¶

    A ten-member, YSA-led Committee with clear, undivided ownership of each Part 4 delivery format; a Learning & Enrichment Coordinator who grounds every offering in members' real interests, talents, and needs; a Facilitator & Mentor Network that carries the four curriculum threads into every small group; and a single reporting chain that keeps the Stake YSA Council informed and able to steer the vision.

    Organization Chart¶

    A companion diagram to the Stake Education and Enrichment Committee Charter and Meeting Plan (10-8-26), showing every seat outlined in Sections 3–4 of the charter.

    Organization chart, rendered as a hierarchy list:

    • YSA Executive Co-Chairs — Vision, direction & approvals — not a Committee seat
    • Committee Co-Chair (Sister)
    • Committee Co-Chair (YSA High Councilor)
      • Committee Core · 10 seats (2 Co-Chairs + 8 Coordinators)
      • Coordinators
        • Monthly Enrichment Night Producer — runs the Monthly Enrichment Night
        • Small-Group Facilitator Coordinator — recruits & trains cross-ward facilitators
        • Workshop & Seminar Series Coordinator — runs the quarterly workshop calendar
        • Discussion Group Coordinator — runs recurring discussion groups
        • Mentorship Pairing Coordinator — pairs YSAs with mature stake mentors
        • Institute & Covenant Progression Liaison — aligns institute topics with curriculum
        • Learning & Enrichment Coordinator — identifies interests, talents & needs
        • Committee Secretary / Metrics Recorder — minutes, attendance & monthly report
    • Married Couple Advisors · Advisory (non-voting)
      • Advisors to the Co-Chairs & Committee
      • Advisors to the Committee
      • Advisors to the Committee
      • Advisors to the Committee
    • Facilitator & Mentor Network · 15–25, seasonal (recruited each curriculum cycle)
      • Small-Group Facilitators — reports to Facilitator Coordinator, for each enrichment night
      • Workshop Presenters — reports to Workshop Coordinator, for each quarterly workshop
      • Discussion Group Hosts — reports to Discussion Coordinator, for each discussion group
      • Mentors — reports to Mentorship Coordinator, for each mentee pairing

    Complete training on teaching standards & confidentiality within two weeks of assignment; report attendance and participation to their Coordinator after every session.

    Totals: 2 Co-Chairs + 8 Coordinators = 10 Committee Core (standing); plus 2–3 Married Couple Advisors (non-voting, advisory); plus a Facilitator & Mentor Network of 15–25 seasonal members (recruited each curriculum cycle — not a fixed seat count).

    Legend: a solid reporting line indicates a direct reporting relationship; a dashed line indicates advisory/oversight — not a reporting line.
