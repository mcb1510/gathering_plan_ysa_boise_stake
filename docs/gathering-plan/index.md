## 2. What We're Trying to Do

- **Our vision:** We want every young single adult in our stake to feel seen, known, and loved — and to feel drawn toward Jesus Christ and sacred covenants.
- **What we mean by "gathering":** reaching and creating a sense of connection and belonging among YSAs with Jesus Christ and each other.
- **What we mean by "reaching":** not just getting active members to show up, but also reconnecting with those who've drifted away, welcoming people who've just moved in, and including friends who aren't members.
- **What we mean by "belonging":** real relationships of trust that come before conversion — where someone's divine identity is affirmed, and covenants feel like an invitation rather than a demand.
- **The idea behind all of this:** every event, league, and class should be a step toward Christ and making and keeping sacred covenants, not just something to do for its own sake.

## 3. How Our Four Priorities Guide Everything We Do

Here's how each stake priority shows up in what we plan:

1. See and Love as God Does → shows up as outreach, checking in on people, and making sure everyone's included.
2. Teach Divine Identity, Purpose, and Potential → shows up as classes and learning nights.
3. Focus on Jesus Christ and the Atonement → shows up as spiritual depth and healing.
4. Invite All to Come unto Christ and Make/Keep Sacred Covenants → shows up as helping people take steps toward covenants.

Simple rule of thumb: before we plan anything, we ask how it connects to one or more of these four priorities, and how it will help a young adult move closer to Christ.

## 4. Where We're Starting From

- **Getting the numbers straight:** comparing ward rosters to who's actually showing up; keeping track of less-active and non-attending YSAs, new move-ins, institute enrollment, and temple recommend holders.
- **Checking in with people:** asking YSAs how connected they feel, what makes them feel isolated, what's keeping them away, and when and how they'd like to gather.
- **Taking stock of what we have:** callings already filled, the institute building, sports equipment, budget, building availability, and chances to team up with other stakes.
- **Finding where people fall through the cracks:** figuring out where someone attends an activity but never takes the next step toward a covenant.

## 5. Priority 1: See and Love as God Does (Reaching Out and Caring for People)

- **Getting to know everyone by name:** like ministering — making sure every YSA on the roster, active or not, gets a personal check-in every month.
- **Reconnecting with less-active YSAs:** our Gathering Specialists team up with ward priesthood and Relief Society leaders to prayerfully figure out whom to reach out to.
- **Welcoming new move-ins:** reach out within 7 days, and personally invite them to a ward or stake activity within 14 days.
- **Making sure no one's left standing alone:** activities are set up so someone who shows up by themselves can instantly connect — greeters, hosts, mixed-ward teams.
- **Using sports to reach out:** open, easy-to-join leagues and pickup games where non-members and less-active YSAs are genuinely welcome.

## 6. Priority 2: Teach Divine Identity, Purpose, and Potential (Learning and Growing Together)

- **What we teach:** who we are as children of God, why we're here, what we can become, getting ready for marriage and family, and being self-reliant.
- **Leaning on the institute:** helping it be a primary gathering place where people learn together, encouraging enrollment, and providing rides to and from class.
- **Monthly classes with rotating topics:** "Who God Says You Are," "Discovering Your Divine Potential," "Being Self-Reliant," "Healthy Relationships and Getting Ready for Marriage."
- **Practical workshops:** career help, budgeting, handling stress, and ways to study scripture — all tied back to the idea that we're meant for something more.
- **Pairing people up:** connecting YSAs with more experienced stake members for advice on life and career.

## 7. Priority 3: Focus on Jesus Christ and the Power of His Atonement (Going Deeper Spiritually)

- **Keeping Christ at the center:** every activity opens and closes with a short reminder of why we're really there, starting with prayer and a brief thought.
- **Making room for healing:** a lot of YSAs are carrying anxiety, loneliness, past mistakes, or grief. We want firesides, small groups, and temple nights to be places where the Atonement's healing power can reach them.
- Regular testimony meetings, "Come, unto Christ" firesides, and sacred music events.
- **Getting to the temple often:** frequent stake temple nights, family history and service opportunities, and helping YSAs get and keep a temple recommend.
- **Music that points to the Savior:** concerts, hymn sings, and devotional music nights.

## 8. Priority 4: Invite Everyone to Come unto Christ and Keep Covenants (Helping People Take the Next Step)

- **Mapping out the next step:** attend → connect → institute → temple → a calling → mission or marriage, helping each YSA determine what's next.
- **Helping friendships lead somewhere:** building relationships that naturally lead people toward baptism or toward active participation.
- **A steady push on temple recommends:** interviews, help getting ready, and group temple trips.
- **Giving people something meaningful to do:** callings and responsibilities that help people feel like they belong and stay committed.
- **Preparation:** classes and mentoring that support YSAs in all aspects of life preparation, education, career, discipleship, and progressing along the covenant path.

## 9. Our Three Main Ways We Gather

**A. Stake Activities (Social)**

- **How often:** one stake-wide activity a month, plus a few bigger events each quarter.
- **Kinds of activities:** socials, service projects, dances, firesides, temple nights, outdoor and adventure events.
- Different wards take turns hosting, so everyone shares ownership and builds friendships across wards.
- **What we're aiming for:** well-planned, starts on time, spiritually grounded, and welcoming to newcomers.

**B. Stake Athletics (Physical)**

- **Leagues:** volleyball, basketball, softball, pickleball, and futsal, with seasons structured to keep people coming back and building real connections.
- **Open-play nights:** an easy, low-pressure way for newcomers and less-active YSAs to jump in.
- **It's about more than the game:** a short sportsmanship devotional, a prayer, and a spiritual thought at every league gathering.
- Tournaments and multi-stake events help people meet even more people and grow their circle.

**C. Learning and Enrichment (Spiritual, Intellectual)**

- The Institute is the hub / Gathering Place for this; see Priority 2.
- Monthly stake enrichment nights, rotating through the four priority themes.
- Workshops and seminars on practical, relational, and spiritual topics.
- Book and article discussion groups, centered on Christ, identity, and purpose.
- Career and life-skills fairs, drawing on the skills already in our stake.

## 10. Who's Doing What

- **Stake Presidency:** designs the plan.
- **Stake YSA Council:** puts the plan into action. Led by two Executive Co-Chairs — the YSA High Councilor and the Stake YSA Relief Society President — its 13-seat Executive Council also includes two Outreach & Shepherding Co-Leads; two Co-Chairs each for Activities, Athletics, and Education & Enrichment; a Gathering Place & Covenant Progression Lead; a Communications & Calendar Lead; and a Council Secretary / Metrics Recorder. Twelve Ward Representatives (an EQ and an RS presidency counselor per ward) complete the 25-member Full Council.
- **Stake YSA Committees:** Activities, Athletics, and Education & Enrichment, each led by two Co-Chairs who sit on the Executive Council, plus the Married Adults Committee.
- **Stake Gathering Specialists:** assisting with identifying, reaching out, and inviting, coordinated through the Outreach & Shepherding Co-Leads.
- **Clear roles for every event:** who's planning it, who's paying for it, who's inviting people, and who's following up.
- **A monthly check-in:** looking at attendance, outreach contacts made, and progress toward covenants (temple recommends, institute enrollment, people becoming active again).

## 11. How We'll Know It's Working

- **Early signs we're on track:** outreach contacts made, visits with less-active members, welcomes to new move-ins, activity and sacrament meeting attendance, institute enrollment, and league participation.
- **Signs of longer-term success:** temple recommend holders, baptisms and reactivations, callings accepted, priesthood ordinations, temple/family history participation, marriages in the stake.
- A quick survey twice a year asking how connected people feel.
- A check-in every few months against the four priorities, adjusting the calendar and our efforts as needed.

## 12. Getting the Word Out

- One shared calendar everyone can see, kept up digitally across wards.
- **A steady rhythm:** a personal invite, then a text reminder, then a group announcement.
- **A mix of channels:** ward announcements, group texts, simple graphics, and personal contact.
- Leaders sharing their own experiences and testimonies to keep the momentum going.

## 13. Budget and Resources

- Putting money first toward the efforts that most clearly help people move closer to Christ.
- Sharing resources across wards, and teaming up with other stakes for sports and big events.
- Keeping costs reasonable so every YSA can take part, regardless of what they can afford.

## 14. A Suggested Timeline

- **Months 1–2:** get a clear picture of where we stand, form the committee, clean up the rosters, and survey how people are feeling.
- **Months 2–3:** build the calendar, launch "Know Them by Name," and kick off the sports seasons.
- **Months 3–6:** run our first learning series, hold our first stake temple night, and host a big quarterly event.
- **Months 6–12:** look at how things are going, tweak what needs tweaking, grow what's working, and start planning year two.

## 15. Keeping This Spiritually Grounded

- Leaders fasting and praying for the YSAs of the stake.
- Regular council meetings, seeking guidance for specific individuals.
- Leaders sharing their own testimonies to set the tone.
- **Following Christ's example:** everything we do seeks to mirror how the Savior ministered; one person at a time, with love, inviting people toward deeper covenants.
