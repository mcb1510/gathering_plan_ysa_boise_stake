# Learning and Enrichment: What We'll Teach

### 1. Why We Teach This

Our learning and enrichment efforts aim to teach divine identity, purpose, and potential (Priority 2), deepen faith in Jesus Christ and His Atonement (Priority 3), and help YSAs move toward sacred covenants (Priority 4). We strive to speak to the whole person: spiritual, intellectual, relational, temporal, and emotional, because divine potential touches every part of life. Everything is built around four topics that mirror our stake priorities.

### 2. Four Big Topics We'll Keep Coming Back To

**Thread 1 — Divine Identity: "Who God Says I Am"**

- **What it's about:** anchoring YSAs in their identity as children of heavenly parents, foreordained with purpose.
- **Why it matters:** it counters the identity confusion, comparison, and anxiety so common among young adults.
- Child of Heavenly Parents: What That Really Means
  - Foreordination and Your Divine Potential
  - Overcoming Comparison Through Divine Identity
  - Worth vs. Worthiness: Understanding Grace
  - Your Divine Nature and the Body
  - Identity in Christ When the World Says Otherwise

**Thread 2 — Divine Purpose: "Why I Am Here"**

- **What it's about:** mortal purpose, the plan of salvation lived out day to day, and each person's unique contribution.
- The Plan of Happiness and Your Daily Life
  - Finding Purpose in Your Season of Life
  - Calling, Career, and Consecration
  - Service as the Path to Joy
  - What God Is Teaching You Right Now
  - Living on the Covenant Path: The Map of Mortality

**Thread 3 — Divine Potential: "Who I Am Becoming"**

- **What it's about:** growth, eternal progression, and preparation for future roles.
- Becoming Like the Savior: The Process of Change
  - Marriage and Eternal Family Preparation
  - Healthy Relationships, Boundaries, and Discernment
  - Emotional Resilience and the Atonement
  - Mental Health, Faith, and Reaching for Help
  - Temporal Self-Reliance: Work, Money, and Providence
  - Developing Spiritual Gifts and Talents

**Thread 4 — Christ and Covenants: "How I Come unto Him"**

- **What it's about:** deepening our relationship with Jesus Christ and progressing in sacred covenants.
- Jesus Christ and the Power of His Atonement (a recurring anchor lesson)
  - Repentance as a Joyful Return, Not a Burden
  - The Sacrament: Weekly Covenant Renewal
  - Temple Covenants and Daily Discipleship
  - Receiving Personal Revelation
  - Enduring Through Christ When Life Is Hard
  - Sharing the Gospel Naturally: Inviting Others to Christ

### 3. Types of Learning Activities

**A. Spiritual and Worship Activities (Christ-Focused)**

- **What it's for:** deepening faith in Jesus Christ (Priority 3) and inviting covenant progression (Priority 4).
- **Examples:** stake testimony meetings, "Come, unto Christ" firesides, devotional concerts and sacred music events, hymn sings, temple nights, scripture studies, Fast Sunday YSA gatherings.
- **How often:** a fireside or devotional each month, plus a stake temple night each quarter.
- **What we make sure happens:** every spiritual activity centers explicitly on the Savior, makes room for personal testimony, and closes with an invitation to take a next step.

**B. Cultural and Enrichment Activities (Identity and Potential-Focused)**

- **What it's for:** reinforcing divine identity, purpose, and potential (Priority 2) through shared cultural and learning experiences.
- **Examples:** cultural celebrations, arts nights, film and discussion evenings, outdoor concerts, museum and outing trips, talent showcases, language and heritage events.
- **How often:** quarterly.
- **What we make sure happens:** each cultural event ties back to one of our learning topics, with a brief teaching moment on identity, purpose, or potential.

### 4. How We'll Teach It

**A. Monthly Stake Enrichment Night**

- **When:** one evening a month, rotating through the four topics.
- **Format:** a brief worship moment (hymn, prayer) → a 35–40-minute lesson → small-group discussion → a personal commitment.
- **Small groups:** cross-ward, 6–10 people, with a trained facilitator; this is where real belonging and connection actually happen.
- **A commitment to close on:** each night ends with a simple, specific invitation, such as reading scripture, visiting the temple, performing an act of service, or making a ministering contact.

**B. Institute Is at the Heart of This**

- Institute is the backbone of enrichment and a Gathering Place, the most effective regular gathering for real doctrinal depth.
- **Our approach:** actively encourage enrollment, remove barriers like rides and scheduling, and line up institute topics with our stake's learning topics.
- **The goal:** every active YSA enrolled in at least one institute course, and every less-active YSA personally invited.

**C. Workshops and Seminars**

- Career development and job-search skills.
- Financial literacy and budgeting.
- Mental and emotional wellness.
- Scripture study and prayer methods.
- Marriage preparation and healthy dating.
- Parenting foundations.
- Taught by our own stake members — a way to connect YSAs to mature, faithful mentors.

**D. Discussion Groups and Book/Article Studies**

- Small, recurring groups (4–8 YSAs) studying a gospel-centered book, general conference talks, or articles.
- **Examples:** studying "Come, Follow Me," recent conference addresses on identity and the Savior, or other approved Church resources.
- **What it offers:** the kind of depth and closeness a big event can't.

**E. Mentorship Pairing**

- YSAs paired with seasoned stake members (couples and individuals) for guidance on life, career, and faith.
- Meets monthly, structured around the four topics.
- Especially valuable for YSAs without nearby family.

### 5. How the Year Flows

- **September–November (Thread 2 — Purpose):** aligning with the back-to-school season and stake conference to help YSAs set their spiritual direction for the year.
- **December (Thread 4 — Christ):** a Christmas-season focus on the Savior and getting ready for the temple and ward life.
- **January–February (Thread 1 — Identity):** a new-year focus on who we are in God's eyes, to help push back against winter discouragement.
- **March–April (Thread 4 — Covenants):** leading into Easter and general conference, with a focus on Christ, the Atonement, and the temple.
- **May–July (Thread 3 — Potential):** getting YSAs ready for summer growth, relationships, careers, service, and the temple.
- **August:** a capstone retreat or testimony night that brings all four topics together.

### 6. How We Teach

- **Doctrine first:** all teaching is grounded in scripture and the words of living prophets; no speculation presented as doctrine.
- **Christ-centered:** every lesson connects back to Jesus Christ and His Atonement.
- **Relevant:** teaching answers real questions YSAs ask and never dismisses doubt, anxiety, or struggle.
- **More conversation, less lecture:** discussion, questions, and real-life application.
- **A safe space:** leaders are trained to listen with love (Priority 1), especially on sensitive topics like mental health, worthiness struggles, and relationships.
- **Trained facilitators:** small-group leaders are briefed on tone, confidentiality, and where to point someone with a serious need.

### 7. How This Ties Back to Our Priorities

- **Priority 1 (See and Love):** enrichment nights are set up so no one sits alone, with facilitators trained to notice and bring in the quiet or the new.
- **Priority 2 (Divine Identity, Purpose, Potential):** the actual content of Threads 1–3.
- **Priority 3 (Christ and the Atonement):** Thread 4, and the spiritual tone of every session.
- **Priority 4 (Invite to Christ and Covenants):** every enrichment night ends with an invitation to take a next step, institute, temple, ministering, or baptism/reactivation.

### 8. How We'll Know It's Working

- Monthly attendance and whether it's trending up.
- Institute enrollment numbers.
- How many small-group facilitators we've trained.
- A survey on how YSAs feel about their own understanding of divine identity, purpose, and potential.
- Signs of covenant progress: temple recommends, how often people say they're studying scripture, reactivations, and callings accepted.
